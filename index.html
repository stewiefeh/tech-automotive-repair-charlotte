What earns the name

Two trades under one roof.

Most shops are good at one of these. TECH Automotive runs both, on both sides of the Atlantic: wrench work and electronics, Detroit steel and German engineering, gasoline and diesel.

MECHANICAL

The wrench work.

Brakes, rotors and hydraulics. Ball joints, tie rods and alignments. Oil pans and valve-cover gaskets. Starters, batteries and everything scheduled maintenance asks for — done to spec, torqued to spec.

ELECTRICAL

The current work.

No-starts, parasitic draws, warning lights that won't quit. We trace the circuit, test the module, and when a control unit is the fault we source it and program it. The work most shops send out, we do in-house.

A BMW that wouldn't start came in with an intermittent no-crank. Diagnosed the same day: a failed control module. Ordered, programmed, running — at a price the owner called reasonable. That's the standard.

Three steps, and you own the decisions.

Check in

Walk in or call. Tell us the sound, the smell, the light on the dash — in your words, not car-part vocabulary. We translate.

2
